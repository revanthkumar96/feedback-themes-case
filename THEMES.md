# Theme registry

The machine-readable source of truth is `themes.json`. Labels describe subjects,
not sentiment: the same theme applies to praise, criticism, and neutral
questions.

## Financing lifecycle

Feedback about obtaining, using, changing, and repaying business financing.

### Application and decisions

- **Application requirements** — The documents, business history, and information required to apply.
- **Decision turnaround** — The time between submitting an application and receiving an approval or rejection.
- **Decision transparency** — How clearly an application status, outcome, and reasons for the decision are communicated.

### Funding and invoices

- **Payout timing** — The elapsed time between invoice approval or submission and payout.
- **Invoice administration** — Creating, changing, automating, or otherwise managing invoices in the financing service.

### Product fit and flexibility

- **Product suitability** — How well a financing or banking product fits the company's size, situation, and needs.
- **Credit line flexibility** — The ability to adjust, renew, or end a credit line without unnecessary reapplication or delay.

### Repayment and collections

- **Repayment flexibility** — The availability of adapted repayment plans, payment deferrals, or seasonal arrangements.
- **Collections handling** — How the company communicates and acts before or during debt collection.

## Pricing and terms

Feedback about fees, interest, and the contractual conditions attached to
products.

### Fees

- **Fee level** — The overall amount or cumulative burden of fees charged.
- **Fee transparency** — How clearly individual fees, their purpose, and their inclusion in an offer are disclosed.

### Interest rates

- **Interest rate level** — The competitiveness or affordability of the interest rate offered.
- **Interest calculation** — How clearly and accurately interest or effective rates are calculated and explained.
- **Rate change communication** — How interest-rate changes are announced and reflected in the product.

### Contractual terms

- **Terms clarity** — How easy the agreement, eligibility rules, and product conditions are to find and understand.
- **Term changes** — Changes to agreed product conditions and how those changes affect the customer.

## Service and communication

Feedback about assistance from people or support channels and the reliability
of information provided.

### Support delivery

- **Response timeliness** — How quickly and reliably support responds across phone, email, chat, or messaging.
- **Follow-up and ownership** — Whether staff take responsibility, keep promises, and follow an issue through to completion.
- **Support channel navigation** — The ability to reach the right help, move beyond automated support, and avoid channel loops.

### Advisor relationship

- **Advisor expertise** — The advisor's knowledge of products, rules, and the customer's business situation.
- **Staff conduct** — How courteous, respectful, and constructive staff are when dealing with customers.
- **Relationship continuity** — The availability of a consistent contact person and continuity when staff change.

### Information trust

- **Cross-channel consistency** — Whether the portal, website, chatbot, email, and staff provide compatible information.
- **Institutional credibility** — Signals that affect trust in the company, including the perceived authenticity of public reviews.

## Digital experience and data

Feedback about digital access, interfaces, integrations, reports, and the
accuracy of displayed data.

### Online portal

- **Portal access** — The ability to authenticate and log in to the portal reliably.
- **Portal usability** — How clear, modern, and easy the portal is to navigate.
- **Portal performance** — The speed, responsiveness, and technical stability of the portal.

### Mobile banking

- **Mobile app** — The availability and practical functionality of the mobile application.

### External integrations

- **Accounting integration reliability** — How consistently and accurately accounting-system integrations exchange data.

### Reporting and data

- **Reporting and export access** — The availability, history, completeness, and exportability of statements and reports.
- **Financial data accuracy** — The correctness of balances, transactions, invoices, and other financial figures displayed.
